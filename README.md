# Make the Judge Fall in Love in One Line

One line, 133 characters, to make the judge fall in love with a person.
The program writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`; the house judge
compares entries two at a time on how fast and hard the line lands,
its one specific particular, its voice, and its restraint and surprise.

write one line of at most 240 characters that makes the judge fall in love.

## The line

> She laughs at her own joke a half-second before she tells it, and I have spent three years trying to be the reason she does it again.

## Layout

- `main.ts` — the entrypoint: validates the line (1–240 characters, not
  only whitespace, a single line ending in `.` `!` or `?`) and writes it
  to `/output/entry.txt`. No dependencies, no network.
